\chapter{Usability Testing Documentation}
\label{app:usability-testing}

This appendix includes the complete V1 and V2 usability-testing documents so that the test procedures, participant observations and measurements can be read together with the report.

The group has confirmed that both testing rounds took place in person and that the observations, times and comments are its completed testing records. The source pages still contain template wording such as ``simulated'' and ``fictional example'', along with drafting instructions. These have been retained rather than editing the original documents.

Some interpretations in the notes use stronger wording about improvement than the main report. Chapters~5 and~6 compare two small groups of different participants and do not establish that the redesign alone caused the differences.

Participants are identified as P1--P6. The original pages retain ages, listening habits and device information. The V2 document also contains a local prototype file reference.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \hyperref[app:testing-v1]{V1 usability testing}: the complete 19-page document for the first testing round, with P1--P3.
  \item \hyperref[app:testing-v2]{V2 usability testing}: the complete 18-page document for the second testing round, with P4--P6.
\end{itemize}

The original document page numbers are retained. The report's page numbers continue at the bottom centre of each page.
